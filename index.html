Step-by-Step Solution

(a) Expanding \(y = -(x^2 - 8x - 9) = -x^2 + 8x + 9\). Since the coefficient of \(x^2\) is negative (\(-1\)), the parabola opens downwards and has a Maximum point.

(b) The x-intercepts are at \(x = -1\) and \(x = 9\). The axis of symmetry lies halfway between them:

\(x = \frac{-1 + 9}{2} = \frac{8}{2} = 4\)

(c) Substitute \(x = 4\) into the equation to find the y-coordinate:

\(y = -(4 + 1)(4 - 9) = -(5)(-5) = 25\)

Thus, the coordinates of the maximum point are (4, 25).
